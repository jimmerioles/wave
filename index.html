About the prototype

A synth you can feel.

Wave is a landing page that behaves like the product. There is no video and no waveform loop — every sound, ring and particle comes from the keys you hold.

Eight voices run through a resonant filter, a soft envelope and a ping-pong echo. The analyser is real, so the orb moves with the notes. The demo is a live arrangement of the chorus of Goodness of God, not a recording.
